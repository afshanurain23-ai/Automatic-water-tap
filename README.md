💧 Automatic Water Tap System

📌 Project Overview

The Automatic Water Tap System is a simple automation project designed to reduce water wastage by controlling the flow of water automatically. It uses a sensor to detect the presence of a person's hand near the tap and activates the water flow without requiring manual operation.

This project promotes water conservation, hygiene, and efficient water usage through automation.

🎯 Objectives

- To reduce water wastage.
- To automate the operation of a water tap.
- To improve hygiene by avoiding direct contact with the tap.
- To understand the practical application of sensors and automation.
- To develop a simple and cost-effective water conservation system.

⚙️ Working Principle

1. The sensor detects a hand placed near the tap.
2. The sensor sends a signal to the control circuit.
3. The control circuit activates the mechanism that allows water to flow.
4. When the hand is removed, the system stops the water flow automatically.

This process helps prevent unnecessary water wastage and makes tap operation more convenient.
